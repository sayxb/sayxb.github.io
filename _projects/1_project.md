Here you can see our stock pitch for SEM 2 25/26. We give a Buy recommendation for AECOM, holding for at least 2 years. To summarise the pitch, we claim that the market is pricing AECOM as a construction company strongly exposed to the industry's cyclical risk. We show qualitative and quantitative evidence that AECOM is successfully diversifying revenue and transitioning to higher margin advisory work and AI-enabled services. We claim that recent lows in AECOM's stock are due to temporary headwinds, which AECOM are well placed to rebound from, with demand set to increase owing to rapid urbanisation and AI infrastructure needs.

We were particularly happy with this piece of work, we felt that it was substantially better than our previous stock pitch. I particularly enjoyed constructing the bootstrapped TMFG networks, which I intended as potential evidence for AECOM's successful diversification, as the number of edges to AECOM had dropped drastically. I regrettably constructed it on raw prices though, rather than squared log returns, which perhaps may have been more reliable. If I were to change one thing it would be that, and I still get a bit annoyed on that oversight to this day.

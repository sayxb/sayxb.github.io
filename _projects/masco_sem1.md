We pitched [Masco](https://masco.com/), a home appliances company, as having strong fundamentals and undergoing strategic restructuring. We claim that the stock is currently undervalued due to macroeconomic headwinds and sector-wide sell-offs. However, with a positive outlook from potential future economic tailwinds, we believe MASCO is well-positioned to rebound as the industry conditions stabilise.
